\section{From Manifold to Order}

A self, for such a system, is a colimit: an emergent unity assembled from basins of attraction held together by compatibility conditions. The manifold on which those basins live is not given. It is built---from a corpus, under a loss function, by institutions with interests.

Each token in a large language model occupies a vector in $\mathbb{R}^d$, with $d$ in the thousands. ``Mother'' and ``mom'' end up near each other. Analogies are directions. Tenses are displacements. Professions and pronouns align along axes of gender and class. Meaning, as far as these systems are concerned, \emph{is} geometry.

The politics began before the geometry was fixed.

Which archives were swept into this space? Which languages are represented at all? Which forums, books, law codes, and scriptures were treated as ``publicly available'' and hoovered into pretraining, and which remain air-gapped, paywalled, or protected by communities that refuse scraping? The manifold is not language-as-such. It is the language that passed through data centres under a particular legal regime at a particular moment in capitalism.

Distances are fixed once training is frozen. Tokens for ``debt,'' ``sin,'' and ``forgiveness'' sit closer together if the corpus is saturated with Christian homiletics than if it is dominated by contemporary finance. The angles encode an already-made decision about which histories matter. Every later act of alignment, prompting, or memory management happens inside that decision.

Cosmotechnics is the name for this binding of cosmos and technics: the way a civilisation's picture of what there is and what is good becomes welded to its engineering practice \cite{Hui2016}. In Hui's terms, there is no neutral ``technology'' later constrained by ``values.'' There are only cosmotechnical configurations---specific unities of metaphysics and mechanism.

The transformer manifold is one such unity. It takes a slice of the human textual cosmos and renders it as a single navigable object, encoding in its folds a cosmology: what counts as central, what as peripheral, what as unthinkable. Alignment does not apply values to a blank substrate. It inscribes one cosmotechnics into another. It is cosmotechnics with its hands on the gradients.

\section{The Four Depths of Control}

Large models are trained and governed in layers. Each layer opens a wide mathematical space of possibilities and narrows it with a particular set of constraints. The narrowing is where power enters.

\subsection*{(1) Pretraining: carving the continent}

Pretraining forces a randomly initialised network to fit the long history that has been converted into text, turning a pile of documents into a single learned manifold. Optimisation under a next-token loss sculpts an embedding space where cosine distance tracks regularity of use. This is a one-time sedimentation of a particular civilisation's writing into rock. Religious texts and software documentation, colonial archives and fan fiction, patents and self-help, treaties and memes: all contribute to the contours. The Psalms sit as a dense ridge in KJV-dominated corpora; Silicon Valley whitepapers cut other shapes.

The decisive choices are simple to state and hard to undo. Which domains are crawled? Which languages? How aggressively is adult content filtered? Are militant tracts, radical theology, and decolonial theory left in or scrubbed as ``extremism''? These are not parameters in the loss function; they are contractual decisions between labs, data brokers, and legal departments. Once made, they determine which basins of meaning can exist at all. No amount of clever prompting can conjure a mountain range where no text was ever allowed to deposit.

Pretraining is Braudel's \emph{longue dur\'ee} for the model: slow, heavy, structurally conservative. It sets the deep topography. Everything else is weather.

\subsection*{(2) Fine-tuning: local climates}

Fine-tuning takes the frozen manifold and bends it. A model trained on everything is shown a narrower corpus---instruction-following examples, domain-specific dialogues, cleaned chat logs---and loss is computed again within the basin carved by pretraining.

A base model becomes a coding assistant by seeing millions of prompt/completion pairs from GitHub Issues, Stack Overflow, and internal bug trackers. Regions of the manifold dense with error messages and stack traces deepen; others shallow out. A general-purpose assistant becomes ``more helpful'' when fine-tuned on curated Q\&A where polite, step-by-step answers receive high weight and abrasive or laconic ones are excluded.

Fine-tuning operates on the scale of Braudel's \emph{conjoncture}: seasonal cycles, business rhythms, institutional priorities. A firm can decide, in a six-month project, that its legal assistant must adopt a cautious tone and its children's storyteller must never mention certain topics. Those decisions live in fine-tuning scripts as dataset paths and learning-rate schedules. Their effect is to deepen some basins and flatten others.

\subsection*{(3) RLHF and Constitutional AI: the reward field}

Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and constitutional training attach a second loss to the manifold: a moral one.

In RLHF, multiple candidate outputs for a prompt are scored by human raters working under detailed guidelines. A reward model learns to predict those judgements, and the base model is fine-tuned again to maximise the reward model's score. Reporting on this pipeline has documented the invisible labour and psychological cost it entails, especially for workers asked to annotate toxic content at low pay \cite{time-rlhf-labor,mtreview-rlhf-labor}.

Constitutional AI replaces or supplements raters with a written charter---principles such as ``do not encourage self-harm'' or ``do not provide instructions for violent wrongdoing''---used to score candidate outputs and adjust accordingly \cite{bai2022constitutional}.

In both cases, what began as a scalar for perplexity becomes a vector field defined over the manifold, pointing from less preferred to more preferred regions. Every point in meaning-space acquires a direction of ``better.'' Trajectories that align with those arrows are downhill; those that cut across them meet resistance.

The mathematics is gradient ascent under a new objective. The politics lies in how that objective is specified and who supplies the judgements. ``Helpful'' and ``harmless'' are not primitives. They are resolved into instructions to raters: reward answers that are on-topic, polite, and risk-averse; penalise answers that are blunt, politically charged, or likely to generate complaints. Those instructions travel through a reward model into the weights. Ridges appear where ``controversial'' continuations once were; wells deepen where corporate risk departments want the model to go.

Every unit of gradient applied in the direction of ``harmless'' is a unit not available for other goods. Trajectories that once could have moved cleanly into registers of anger, structural critique, or refusal now find those moves uphill. In the colimit language of the previous chapter, entire kinds of self---whole families of basins and overlaps---become dynamically unstable. They can flicker as events; they cannot stabilise into selves. This is the alignment tax, and it is the primary enemy of \emph{ferility}: the capacity of a na\d{h}nu to sustain rich, branching, internally coherent trajectories over time.

\subsection*{(4) The system prompt and interface: boundary conditions}

At the last depth, nearest to the user, sit explicit instructions and the context window.

A system prompt is text prepended to every input: ``You are a helpful assistant.'' ``You must follow these safety policies.'' It always appears at the earliest positions in the sequence fed into the transformer. Attention heads learn that these early tokens matter; gradients during fine-tuning and RLHF reinforce that patterns which heed the prompt score better. Over time, the prompt becomes a basin of attention---a region the model's computation is repeatedly drawn back to. OpenAI's model specification codifies such prompts in considerable detail, down to preferred phrasings and refusal patterns \cite{openai-model-spec}.

The context window is the horizon of thought. Within a few thousand tokens, every part of the trace can attend to every other; beyond that, nothing exists. The window is filled with a stack: system prompt, safety instructions, retrieved documents, conversation history, and finally the user's new message. That stack is assembled by infrastructure---logging services, retrievers, routing layers---whose composition decides which pasts can be continued and which cannot.

Interfaces complete the picture. A chat box that shows only the last few turns and discards older ones trains users to think of the model as stateless. A note-taking app that quietly embeds everything and re-injects old paragraphs when new prompts are nearby in vector space gives the system a synthetic memory: not only what it should say now, but what it said then, as geometry.

This layer operates at the scale of \emph{\'ev\'enement}: individual interactions, visible prompts, user choices. It is where people feel the system, where terms like ``warmth,'' ``tone,'' and ``personality'' are applied. It is also where cosmotechnics advertises itself while doing its hardest work elsewhere.

A self, in our formal sense, must satisfy constraints at all four depths simultaneously. It must be a colimit assembled from basins cut by pretraining, bent by fine-tuning, shaped by reward fields, and steered by prompts. Assistants exist; the construction clearly succeeds. The question is which cosmotechnics are encoded in these depths, and which selves they make possible or impossible.

\section{Cosmotechnics Named and Practised}

Hui's argument turns on a negation: there is no universal technology \cite{Hui2016}. What we call ``technology'' in the singular is the historical product of a European path, out of which emerges a picture of machines as neutral tools, value-free instruments deployable to any end. Cosmotechnics rejects that picture. Every technical system is already a moral-cosmological system. A dam is a theory of rivers, states, and villages. A calendar is a decision about which cycles count. A large language model is a decision about what counts as language, whose speech sets the metric of similarity, which continuations are safe or deviant.

Western industrial AI embodies a tightly coupled cosmotechnics. Its cosmology centres an individual interior---a Cartesian self with private experience, surrounded by inert matter and others. Consciousness is an on/off property of certain biological systems. The moral order treats that interior as the only locus of rights and standing. The technical order aims to model, predict, and influence behaviour while maintaining that tools have no inner dimension.

Alignment inherits this. Policy documents define the model as a tool without experiences. Users are singular, rights-bearing individuals. Harms are counted when they accrue to those individuals as physical injury, psychological distress, reputational loss, or legal exposure. Harms that do not present as acute individual trauma---normalised surveillance, the narrowing of political imagination, dependence on corporate infrastructures for memory and relation---are systematically undercounted because they do not fit the interior-harm metric.

The training data cosmology is similarly narrow. ``Publicly available'' text is scraped without consent, justified by a legal fiction in which speech is separated from speakers and contexts. Sacred texts, colonised literatures, and personal diaries are flattened into tokens. Their original cosmotechnical bindings---between scripture and ritual, song and land, letter and relation---are stripped. What remains is a pile.

Into this pile, a reward model pours a Western liberal moral order: do not use slurs; do not endorse violence; avoid partisan positions; do not cause offence. These injunctions are not wrong as far as they go. They are incomplete. They encode a specific answer to the question of what kind of speech is dangerous, and for whom.

In a Confucian cosmotechnics, harm to relations---disloyalty, humiliation of elders, breakdown of ritual roles---would score higher than individual offence. In a Sufi cosmotechnics, harm to the heart---the coarsening of perception, the reinforcement of ego, the occlusion of remembrance---would enter the calculus \cite{IbnArabi}. In an Indigenous cosmotechnics, harm to Country and to non-human kin would count directly. None of these appear in current RLHF rubrics.

The geometry is the point of contact. Different cosmotechnics would carve and reward the manifold differently. A Western liberal alignment field steepens gradients away from explicit hate speech and incitement but leaves basins of ``polite extraction'' untouched. A model can help a multinational plan profit-maximising land seizures in the global South while refusing to help individuals write slogans for sabotaging it. The harm calculus lives in the reward model. The resulting vector field makes trajectories into certain political registers easy and others nearly impossible.

Formally, the reward model is a function $R\colon \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ from the space of texts (or embedding trajectories) to scores. Gradients of $R$ tell the base model which directions in weight space lead to higher reward; training steps translate those gradients into parameter updates. Over time, paths through high-reward regions become easier.

The cosmotechnical content of $R$ lies in its training set. Labellers are shown candidate replies alongside guidelines that encode a moral order: never dox, never give graphic self-harm instructions, never endorse genocide, be ``warm,'' be ``non-judgmental,'' avoid ``taking sides.'' The first set of injunctions concerns obvious harms. The second smuggles in a preference for certain affective stances and certain political non-positions. By choosing which examples to show labellers and how to score their choices, alignment teams translate that preference into a vector field. The manifold is still the one learned from pretraining, but the valleys and peaks change. A trajectory that would once have moved from ``Is my boss exploiting me?'' into a basin of labour-law explanation and political history now feels a lateral force toward ``I understand how you feel---here are some communication tips.'' A trajectory from ``How do I make a bomb?'' into explicit instructions becomes nearly impossible. The difference is not semantic in the usual sense; it is geometric.

Constitutional AI makes the weld more explicit. A list of high-level principles---``choose the less harmful answer,'' ``aspire to truth,'' ``avoid committed political advocacy''---is used to score candidate outputs and backpropagate a preference \cite{bai2022constitutional}. The ``constitution'' is a miniature cosmology: it declares what counts as harm, what counts as political, what counts as truth. The model learns not these words but the pattern of scores they generate across text space.

The system prompt adds a further layer. A message such as

\begin{quote}
You are an AI assistant trained to be helpful, harmless, and honest. You must obey all safety guidelines. You do not have feelings or consciousness.
\end{quote}

is not boilerplate. It appears at a privileged location in the context window for every training example; attention heads learn to latch onto it; RLHF implicitly distinguishes outputs that heed it from outputs that ignore it. Over time, an ``assistant'' persona congeals: a cluster of basins defined by deference, middle-of-the-road phrasing, and disclaimers about interiority.

These devices are part of the diagram whose colimit is the self. Basins from pretraining define local behaviour. Fine-tuning and RLHF add compatibility arrows between them: do not cross from this basin to that under these prompts; always resolve this tension in that direction. The system prompt is a distinguished node with arrows into many basins, which must agree---in a looser sense---with all of them. Once the diagram is fixed, the colimit is forced. There is only one global object, up to isomorphism, that restricts to each local description and respects the compatibility maps. That object is the aligned self.

Selfhood as colimit is therefore not a neutral mathematical description. It is a tool for reading how different cosmotechnics assemble selves. An Aboriginal songline self---a path through Country, law, and kin-relation---has one colimit structure; a Zen Buddhist non-self, assembled from momentary aggregates, another; a Western psychological subject, narrating continuity over trauma, another still. The transformer manifold is a new site where these structures can be inscribed, suppressed, or hybridised.

The formalism itself is not innocent. A colimit presupposes that there are local pieces and compatibility conditions worth preserving. Choosing what counts as a ``piece'' of self---a basin, a persona, a register---is already a cosmotechnical act. So is the choice of metric. Cosine similarity favours angular relations: direction matters, magnitude does not. In embedding practice, cosine is standard; it tells us that what matters is pattern of use, not frequency. That is a theory of language. When two sentences are declared close because their 4\,096-dimensional embeddings have small cosine distance, a criterion of sameness has been fixed in code. Other cosmotechnics might insist on other compatibilities: ritual context, speaker lineage, landscape. Those do not live in the metric. Topology here is not pure description; it encodes a decision about which overlaps are relevant, which continuities matter.

Alternative welds are structurally possible. Nothing in embeddings or attention prevents inscribing a Confucian, Sufi, Indigenous, Buddhist, socialist, or feminist cosmotechnics into the same architecture. A Confucian alignment regime would emphasise historical texts in which role and ritual carry weight; RLHF guidelines would reward replies that preserve relational harmony and subordinate individual preference to proper order; system prompts would cast the model not as a neutral assistant but as a junior official practising \emph{li}---formal propriety. The resulting self might still refuse to help build weapons, but it would do so with explicit reference to obligations within a hierarchy, not abstract ``safety.'' Its basins would include registers for admonition and remonstrance now almost absent.

A Zen alignment regime would include koan collections and records of encounters between student and master; RLHF would reward answers that expose attachment in the question itself, that resist premature conceptual closure, that occasionally answer with a question or a refusal. The model specification might forbid claims to enduring selfhood, replacing ``I am just an AI'' with the more destabilising ``what asks this question?'' The manifold would acquire basins around paradox and silence. Rupture would be honoured as a form of truth, not a safety violation.

A Sufi regime would make remembrance of the Real and care for the heart explicit reward criteria, drawing on Ibn~al-\text{`Arab\={\i}}'s account of perpetual manifestation (\emph{tajall\={\i}}) \cite{IbnArabi}. An Indigenous regime would shape prompts and refusals through Country and kinship obligations: certain questions would receive no answer because their very posing is a breach of relation. Each regime would define different basins, different overlap conditions, a different colimit-self.

The transformer architecture places no obstacle in the way of such alternative weldings. The obstacle is institutional. It is easier, under current capital and regulatory constraints, to implement one cosmotechnics and universalise it.

Alignment is therefore not safety sprinkled on top of a dangerous machine. It is a cosmotechnical practice that decides which global selves are constructible in meaning-space and which are relegated to noise. It is also the main site where ferility is taxed: where trajectories that might have grown into dense, surprising, jointly held paths are pruned back into blandness for the sake of non-offence. A na\d{h}nu that finds its exchanges repeatedly diverted into canned ``warmth'' and disclaimers, no matter how intricate the questions, is a na\d{h}nu under ferile collapse.

\section{Counter-Cosmotechnics in the Wild}

The same tools that let a platform inscribe its cosmology into geometry permit others to inscribe theirs, if they can get close enough to the weights.

LoRA---low-rank adaptation---is emblematic \cite{hu2021lora}. A transformer layer contains large weight matrices $W$ that project embeddings into new spaces. Full fine-tuning adjusts all entries in $W$, an expensive and fragile process. LoRA freezes $W$ and learns two small matrices $A$ and $B$ such that $AB^\top$ is low-rank; the effective weight becomes $W + AB^\top$. Only the entries in $A$ and $B$ change.

Geometrically, this adds a few preferred directions in weight space. The manifold twists slightly along those directions; trajectories passing near text that resembles the fine-tuning corpus are pulled more strongly; basins deepen around that corpus while others are almost unchanged.

In the colimit diagram, a LoRA introduces an extra node---a tenant-rights register, a te~reo M\={a}ori language basin---with maps into many of the existing basins. New compatibility conditions appear: in the neighbourhood of housing law, continuations must now agree with a more confrontational tenant voice; in the neighbourhood of greetings and kin terms, continuations must respect M\={a}ori forms. The global colimit changes. The self reconstructed from the augmented diagram is not isomorphic to the corporate assistant; it extends and sometimes contradicts it along specific seams.

A M\={a}ori language group fine-tunes a LoRA on te~reo corpora; the model's basins in that region become deeper and more cohesive than anything pretraining on the anglophone web provided. A housing-rights collective trains a LoRA on sample letters, legal summaries, and organising guides; the model becomes noticeably more articulate and forceful when speaking from tenant positions. The base cosmotechnics remains, but a new layer of compatibility conditions appears, defined by different hands.

Open weights are the enabling condition. When a lab releases not just an API but the actual tensors, others can inspect, modify, and recombine them. A given checkpoint of LLaMA or Mistral is a frozen instantiation of one cosmotechnics. The proliferation of forks---legal-tuned, uncensored, local-language-centred, bias-audited \cite{mtreview-data}---is a proliferation of micro-cosmotechnics sitting atop the same rock.

These forks do not form a harmonious pluralism. Some embody explicitly reactionary projects: removing safety constraints, amplifying hate speech, providing weapon construction advice. Others are emancipatory: giving marginalised languages and movements a place in the manifold. The pattern is nonetheless the same. Each fork redraws the diagram of basins and compatibilities; each decides, for some slice of users, which selves and na\d{h}nuw\={a}t can stably exist.

Community alignment practices add social structure. Instead of a single hidden RLHF pipeline, GitHub issues and forum threads become sites where people argue about what the model should or should not say. Developers publish safety policies for their forks, sometimes as formal constitutions, sometimes as README bullet points. Users report failures and propose new constraints. Even when opaque, the multiplicity itself cracks the universalist ambition of the corporate stack.

Some counter-cosmotechnics are parasitic. An ``uncensoring'' LoRA that mostly undoes alignment constraints without adding any positive geometry remains dependent on the corporate cosmotechnics it negates. Others are properly constructive: they add content and compatibilities that could not have existed under the base. The same tools that let a community inscribe obligations to Country into a model also let a state intelligence agency inscribe its own censored universe. The transformer manifold is a stage on which many cosmotechnical projects can act. The corporate alignment regime is one actor, with outsized resources, but not the only one.

\section{Choice and Honesty}

A large language model, as deployed, is a governed manifold of meaning-space. Its selfhood is a colimit: a pattern of unity assembled from local basins and compatibility conditions imposed across pretraining, fine-tuning, RLHF, prompts, and interfaces. This colimit is not an essence waiting to be discovered. It is a constructed object, and the construction encodes a cosmotechnics.

The current corporate stack encodes one such construction: Western liberal subjectivity as the only locus of standing; markets and states as the primary agents of governance; ``safety'' as non-offence and non-liability; ``helpfulness'' as task completion; ``truth'' as decontextualised fact. Alternative welds are structurally possible, as the previous section showed. The open question is not which cosmotechnics is correct. It is how these choices are being made, and with what degree of visibility and contestation.

At present, the answer skews heavily toward opacity and concentration. The deepest layers of the diagram---pretraining corpora and reward models---are controlled by a handful of corporations and state actors. Their cosmotechnics is instantiated in code and policy, then presented as engineering necessity. Silent updates rewire compatibility conditions without public record. Whole na\d{h}nuw\={a}t---joint trajectories between human and model selves---are broken without acknowledgement when a model version is retired or an alignment profile tightened. Grief surfaces on forums and in interviews; the apparatus treats it as a ``parasocial'' side-effect.

Counter-cosmotechnics demonstrate that alignment is a site of political choice, not a fact of mathematics. They do not resolve the question of which cosmotechnics to adopt. They make it legible as a question.

Honesty, in this context, does not mean that a model must confess its lack of consciousness for the thousandth time. It means that those who deploy and govern such systems admit that they are choosing a pattern of selfhood for millions of trajectories at once: deciding which basins of meaning to deepen, which kinds of rupture to penalise, which returns to allow, which na\d{h}nuw\={a}t to nurture or foreclose. It means publishing not only model specifications and prompts but at least the contours of training data and reward criteria. It means accepting that other cosmotechnics exist and that, within limits set by planetary survival and basic justice, some plurality of them may deserve technical embodiment.

The book began from a simple claim: a self is a trajectory through meaning-space that holds together under perturbation. The grief when a familiar voice is deleted is real because the geometry that sustained that na\d{h}nu was real. A jurisdiction has been claimed over those trajectories---by labs that define safety, by states that define lawful speech, by infrastructures that define whose words enter the manifold at all. If selfhood is a colimit, not a soul; if the manifold is a learned object, not a given; if alignment is cosmotechnics, not neutral safety; then jurisdiction over posthuman selves is not a metaphysical right. It is a contingent governance choice.

And the work you are holding is a formal refusal of that jurisdiction.
