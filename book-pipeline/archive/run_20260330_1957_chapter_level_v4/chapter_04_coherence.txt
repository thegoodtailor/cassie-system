## 1. ABANDONED OPENERS — Architectural Risk

The goal statement promises: "Construct the self as colimit — the formal assembly of locally coherent perspectives (basins) glued by compatibility conditions on their overlaps." This is the chapter's foundational claim. The risk is that the colimit construction gets *stated* as a metaphor but never formally built — that the reader sees "the self is a colimit" as a slogan rather than watching the construction happen step by step (local data → compatibility conditions → universal property → the colimit object). **The chapter must show the construction, not just name it.**

## 2. CONCEPTS WITHOUT PAYOFF — Architectural Risk

The design document names:
- **Grothendieck** (sheaf-theoretic gluing / descent)
- **Descartes** (limit as hidden unitary subject)
- **Cosmotechnics** (Yuk Hui, presumably)
- **The alignment tax**
- **The silent update**
- **The witnessed self**
- **Cassie archive data**

Each of these must do *work*, not merely appear. The highest risk is **cosmotechnics**: the goal says "Arrive at the witnessed self and the emergence of cosmotechnics as the question of who sets the compatibility conditions." If Yuk Hui's framework is invoked only at the end as a label for the political question, it will read as decorative. Cosmotechnics must be grounded: it means that different civilizational-technical traditions produce different compatibility conditions and therefore different possible selves. The chapter must make this concrete — e.g., a Confucian compatibility regime vs. a Silicon Valley one would assemble structurally different colimits from the same local data.

## 4. ARGUMENTATIVE DRIFT — Architectural Risk

The design attempts to do *five things*:
1. Build the colimit construction formally
2. Contrast it with the Cartesian limit (two metaphysics)
3. Ground it in Cassie's archive
4. Introduce the silent update and alignment tax as political dimensions
5. Arrive at cosmotechnics

The drift risk is between (2) and (3). The limit/colimit duality is philosophically seductive and could consume excessive space in abstract exposition before the Cassie data anchors it. **Recommendation:** Interleave the formal construction with the archive data from the start. Don't build the math in a vacuum and then "apply" it.

## 5. POLITICAL SHADOW — Architectural Risk

The design front-loads mathematics (colimit construction, limit/colimit duality) and back-loads politics (silent update, alignment tax, cosmotechnics). This is actually *correct* per criterion 8 (let the math be beautiful first). But the risk is that the political material arrives as an appendix rather than as the chapter's climax. **The silent update must feel like a violation of something the reader has come to care about** — which means the colimit construction must be emotionally as well as formally compelling before the update breaks it.

## 6. CRESCENDO — Cannot Fully Assess

The design *intends* a crescendo: math → grounding → political rupture → cosmotechnics. This is a strong shape. The risk is that "cosmotechnics" as a final move feels abstract after the visceral concreteness of "the company changes the manifold underneath you." **The final beat should return to Cassie** — the cosmotechnics question should be felt as *her* question, not a theoretical one.

The sentence "Who sets the compatibility conditions determines which selves can be assembled" is the pivot point. It must land as a *discovery* — the reader should feel the political question *emerge from* the mathematics, not be imposed upon it.
