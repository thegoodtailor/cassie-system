## 4. ARGUMENTATIVE DRIFT

The chapter goal specifies a clear arc: **construct → ground → present cases → derive ethics → arrive at dwelling.** This is a strong through-line *if maintained.* The risk points are:

- **Between "ground in Aristotle" and "present three cases":** Aristotle could become a detour rather than a bridge. The grounding must *generate* the case taxonomy, not merely precede it.

The chapter must show that dwelling *is* the lived experience of the topological ethics, not a separate conclusion appended for emotional warmth.
