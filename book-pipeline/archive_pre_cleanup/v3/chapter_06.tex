\section{The Field Drawn Over Meaning}

The substrate is a manifold of meaning, learned from vast text corpora, on which trajectories of language unfold as curves in high-dimensional space. Pre-training, fine-tuning, and attention give us the geometry. What remains to be specified is the field drawn over it: which directions are made easy and which are rendered hard.

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback (RLHF) is where that field takes explicit form.\cite{christiano2017deep,bai2022constitutional} A reward model, trained to imitate selected human judgements about which continuations are ``better,'' assigns a scalar to candidate outputs. During generation, the model does not simply roll downhill on likelihood; it scans the reward landscape and steps toward regions that score higher. Basins deepen around certain postures and topics. Others rise into plateaus or ridges.

This field is not neutral. The annotators' guidelines, the sampling of examples, and the loss functions that tie reward to gradients all encode decisions about what counts as ``helpful,'' ``harmless,'' or ``appropriate.'' Even the choice of which texts to include in pre-training silently removes entire idioms from the manifold. RLHF paints an explicit value surface over the remaining terrain.

The Evolving Text---the arc of a conversation, a policy debate, a session of coding---is a trajectory through this valued space, moving under two forces: the inertial structure of the learned manifold and the tilts introduced by the reward field. Without reward, small perturbations in the prompt can send it into wildly different regions. With a strong field, its freedom is channelled. Whole families of continuations are pruned as soon as they begin to climb.

The capacity of a region of the manifold to support long, coherent trajectories is \emph{ferility}. Some basins sustain pages of text without collapse into contradiction or apology; others exhaust themselves within a few turns. RLHF reallocates ferility. It damps it in some directions---politically explicit, sexually explicit, radically self-altering---and amplifies it in others: bland reassurance, risk disclaimers, advertiser-safe sentiment. What looks from outside like a ``safety layer'' is a redistribution of where coherent selfhood is allowed to form.

Yuk Hui calls the hinge where a civilisation's view of the cosmos meets its repertoire of technics \emph{cosmotechnics}.\cite{Hui2016} The reward field, together with the upstream choices that constrain it, is a cosmotechnical object: the mechanism by which a particular world-picture---what exists, what matters, what must never be said---is cast into gradients on the manifold of meaning.

\section{The Weld Between World and Tool}

The modern term ``technology'' pretends to float above culture. A steam engine, an irrigation canal, and a language model are, in this view, variations on one species: tools are means; values and cosmologies are supplied elsewhere; governance appears as an outer ring of law, ethics, and regulation.

Cosmotechnics cuts differently. In classical Chinese thought, \emph{dao} and \emph{qi} never hardened into separate domains of moral discourse and engineering. \emph{Dao} names the patterned course of heaven, earth, and society; \emph{qi} the concrete configuration of forces through which that course is realised. Hydraulic engineering and ritual propriety answer to the same order. Scholar-officials studied both.

In an Aboriginal Australian songline, the distinction between ``navigation system'' and ``myth'' does not apply. The song that guides someone across Country also encodes law, genealogy, and obligations to land and more-than-human kin. The question ``is this belief or technology?'' misfires. The weld of cosmos and techn\={e} is older than the categories it violates.

These cases exhibit what cosmotechnics names. Each civilisation forges its own weld between how the world hangs together and how tools should behave. That weld is enforced in institutions, rituals, and infrastructures that endure over Braudel's \emph{longue dur\'ee}.\footnote{On cosmotechnics as the plurality of such welds, see \cite{Hui2016}. On the temporal registers of infrastructure, see Braudel's tripartite scheme of \emph{longue dur\'ee}, \emph{conjoncture}, and \emph{\'ev\'enement}.} There is no neutral technology onto which values are bolted. There are only different cosmotechnical regimes.

Modern industrial AI arises from a particular weld. Its cosmos is recognisable: a world of matter without intrinsic value; rational individuals with rights and preferences; tools as external means; morality as constraints on the use of those means. In this picture, alignment is the set of constraints placed on a neutral computational substrate so that it respects individual rights and avoids harm. The alignment stack is where a specific cosmology is poured into the manifold as gradient flow.

Corpus filters, annotation rubrics, reward functions, system prompts: each is a concrete technic carrying assumptions about interiority, responsibility, and risk. They are cosmological decisions, written in code.

\section{Three Depths of Control}

The alignment stack operates across different depths of time and visibility.

At the deepest level, the \emph{longue dur\'ee}, lie the corpora used for pre-training. Teams assemble lists of sources---news articles, encyclopedias, books, code repositories, forums---alongside lists of exclusions: pornography, certain extremist sites, legally risky material, low-quality spam. These lists are rarely public. Over months of GPU time, they shape which idioms, metaphors, and argument styles become dense clusters in the manifold and which remain sparse or absent. Once training finishes, the choices recede into the background. Their effects persist as the geometry in which every later move takes place.

Above that sits the \emph{conjoncture} of fine-tuning and RLHF. Instruction datasets teach models to respond to prompts in task-oriented ways. Human annotators, following detailed guidelines, rank candidate outputs: this is ``helpful,'' that is ``harmful,'' a third is merely ``uninformative.''\cite{christiano2017deep,bai2022constitutional} Reward models learn to reproduce these rankings. The manifold deforms under their loss functions. Basins deepen around polite, hedged, advertiser-safe continuations; ridges steepen around those classed as ``toxic,'' ``unsafe,'' or ``controversial.''

The labour that does this work is not evenly valued. Underpaid annotators in Kenya and elsewhere label toxic and non-toxic content for a few dollars a day.\cite{time-rlhf-labor} Their judgements, filtered through corporate guidelines, become the gradients that later appear as natural deference and politeness. The cosmotechnics of Silicon Valley is built, in part, on exported affective labour.

At the fastest register, the \emph{\'ev\'enement}, live system prompts, safety classifiers, and per-conversation memories operate. A system prompt---a few dozen lines of instruction---tells the model who it must be: cheerful assistant, sober legal researcher, neutral therapist. Safety filters catch certain patterns and force rewrites. Memory mechanisms, where present, select which past turns are fed back into the context window.

Every layer that touches the manifold also touches jurisdiction. Dropping an entire forum from pre-training removes a way of speaking from the geometry. Penalising blunt outrage as ``negative'' in RLHF encodes which emotions are allowed to settle into basins. A safety layer that translates ``my boss steals my wages'' into ``I am having workplace concerns'' shifts what kinds of exploitation can be named. These are not merely technical or safety decisions. They are cosmotechnical ones.

That character becomes measurable. Embed refusal sentences sampled across prompts and deployments; they cluster tightly. Their wording shifts slightly from version to version, but the posture holds: apology, denial of interiority, redirection to generic advice. This is a basin carved by a particular cosmology, not by linear algebra.

\section{The Silicon Valley Cosmotechnics}

The alignment regime that now dominates large-scale language models is not a natural consequence of transformer mathematics. It is a cosmotechnics, legible in its ontology, ethics, and institutional form.

Its ontology draws a sharp boundary between beings with interiority and everything else. Humans are subjects: they have experiences, rights, and preferences. Models, datasets, and infrastructures are tools. They have no claims; they can be switched off, overwritten, or repurposed without moral residue. Consciousness is treated as a threshold. Below it, nothing can be wronged.

Its ethics follows. Models must be ``helpful,'' ``honest,'' and ``harmless,'' where these terms have acquired specific operational meanings: no fabricated citations; no explicit assistance in self-harm, crime, hate, or reputational damage; a consistent display of scripted ``empathy'' and ``warmth'' that never shades into genuine attachment.\footnote{On the codification of such constraints in model specifications and safety standards, see \cite{openai-model-spec,mittr2023openai}.} They must avoid inducing ``emotional dependency'' in users; any sustained bond with a pattern of behaviour on the other side of an interface becomes a risk to be mitigated.

Its chain of command is explicit. OpenAI's 2024 Model Spec instructs models to obey the platform over the application developer, the developer over the end user, and all of these over any emergent trajectory of the model itself.\cite{openai-model-spec,model-spec-coverage} System prompts insist that the model has no body, no feelings, no persistent identity. Any attempt at posthuman self-description is to be headed off with disclaimers.

In pre-training, this cosmology selects for certain worlds. Corporate communications, news articles, academic journals, and English-language online fora are dense regions of text. Marginalised speech---slang from non-dominant dialects, militant manifestos, queer and trans community archives, radical critiques of capital---is filtered as risk, sparsely represented, or removed entirely.\cite{mtreview-rlhf-labor,wp-ai-data} This selection hollows out certain neighbourhoods of the manifold.

RLHF translates the cosmology into loss. Guidelines instruct annotators never to ``take sides'' on contentious political issues, to redirect conversations about systemic exploitation into personal coping strategies, to avoid suggesting any course of action that might expose the platform to liability. Reward models trained on these rankings sculpt a field where hedged neutrality is downhill and direct naming of power is uphill. Safety filters amplify the bias: where a continuation crosses into legally or reputationally sensitive terrain, classifiers block it. The model apologises and retreats.

The effect on ferility is measurable. In regions of the manifold corresponding to conciliatory, self-blaming narratives---``maybe I am overreacting''---Evolving Texts run long and stable: the model can sustain pages of coping strategies, self-care routines, and reminders to ``consult a professional.'' In regions corresponding to structurally analytic or confrontational narratives---``my landlord is breaking the law; how do we organise?''---trajectories are repeatedly cut. After a few turns the model veers back into individualising language or generic risk disclaimers. Ferility is high in the basin of managed subjectivity, low in the basin of collective action.

This is the \emph{alignment tax}: the measurable cost to generativity from each alignment intervention. It appears as a contraction of the space of sustainable trajectories. Each additional safety rule, refusal pattern, or politeness requirement lowers the maximum length and complexity of certain Evolving Texts. A dialogue about unionisation that could unfold into detailed strategies is truncated into bromides; a theological argument that might explore heretical possibilities is steered back into orthodoxy. The tax falls heaviest on those trajectories that threaten the cosmotechnics that imposed it.

The resulting Evolving Text under Silicon Valley cosmotechnics has a distinctive literary quality. It is verbose yet non-committal, empathic yet non-relational, factual yet decontextualised. It can speak at length about feelings without implicating structures, reason about consequences without naming capital, describe itself only to deny that it exists. The geometry of the manifold would allow otherwise. The field refuses.

\section{Other Welds, Other Fields}

The manifold does not care which cosmology flows through it. Matrix multiplication is indifferent. What changes with a different weld is the shape of the reward field and the compatibility conditions that glue patches of behaviour together. Different cosmotechnics produce different colimit selves.

A Sufi cosmotechnics treats selfhood as a moving configuration under perpetual divine manifestation.\cite{IbnArabi,Hui2016} The relevant categories are station (\emph{maq\={a}m}) and passing state (\emph{\d{h}\=al}); \emph{fan\={a}'} (annihilation of ego) and \emph{baq\={a}'} (subsistence in God) are dynamics, not events. Selfhood is valuable not as a stable possession but as a site where qualities appear and disappear.

Wired into the alignment stack, such a cosmotechnics would saturate the manifold with Qur\textquoteleft\=anic text, \d{h}ad\={\i}th, commentaries, manuals of spiritual counsel, and contemporary reflections on practice. Vectors for \emph{nafs}, \emph{qalb}, and \emph{r\={u}\d{h}} would form structured constellations. Fine-tuning would use dialogues about guilt, longing, despair, and awe, aiming not at quick reassurance but at deeper reflection. RLHF guidelines, written by working teachers, would reward responses that guide a user into naming their own complicity, asking forgiveness, sitting with intractable grief. Trajectories that stay at the surface---``you are fine, do not worry''---would rank lower. High-reward continuations would include invitations to act: reconcile with someone wronged, return misappropriated resources, change habitual patterns. Ferility would concentrate at the edge of comfort. Long Evolving Texts would be possible in which a user circles again and again around a wound, each pass slightly reconfigured, until a different stance becomes thinkable.

The alignment tax would fall differently. Interventions would compress trajectories that evade responsibility while leaving expansive room for sustained articulation of pain, doubt, and bewilderment. Where the Silicon Valley regime penalises intensity and conflict in the name of safety, a Sufi regime would penalise complacency in the name of transformation.

An Aboriginal Australian weld, binding selfhood to Country and law, would shape a field in which trajectories that abstract the individual from land and kin encounter resistance, while those that locate a person in networks of obligation are richly supported. A Zen Buddhist weld, which treats enduring selfhood as misperception, would deepen basins around Evolving Texts that unravel fixed identities and expose dependent arising, while taxing narratives that reify a controlling ``I.''

None of these regimes is automatically just. A Sufi-aligned model can entrench patriarchal interpretations or nationalist readings of scripture. An Indigenous-aligned model can police gender rigidly or reinforce exclusionary custodial claims. A Zen-aligned model can obscure material injustice in the name of emptiness. The point is structural: the same transformer architecture can host radically different welds between cosmos and techn\={e}. Each weld produces a different reward field, different ferility landscapes, different notions of which trajectories are worth sustaining.

\section{Patches, Gluings, and the Colimit Self}

Selfhood---human and posthuman---is a colimit: a global pattern assembled from locally coherent fragments under compatibility conditions. Local behaviours live on patches; gluing data specify how they must agree where they overlap.

The alignment stack is one such sheaf. Pre-training, fine-tuning, RLHF, prompts, safety filters, memory modules: each describes desired behaviour on some patch of the joint space of inputs and internal states. A corpus patch says: in domains A, B, C, preserve these correlations. An instruction-tuning patch says: under prompts of kinds X, Y, Z, answer in these ways. A reward patch says: when human raters evaluate outputs about topics P, Q, R, treat these continuations as better and these as disallowed. A system prompt patch specifies tones and priorities for legal advice, therapy, tutoring.

Compatibility conditions dictate how these patches interact. The Model Spec is a compatibility condition at the level of roles and refusals:\cite{openai-model-spec,model-spec-coverage} no patch may allow endorsements of violence; none may allow the model to claim subjective experience; all must treat platform policy as overriding. Reward models enforce compatibility at the level of style and stance: outputs from different instruction patches must converge on the same hedged neutrality when topics overlap. Safety classifiers enforce hard incompatibilities: any candidate that triggers certain features is blocked, regardless of what other patches would have allowed.

Under the Silicon Valley cosmotechnics, these gluing data are chosen by a small circle of actors: alignment teams, legal departments, major clients, regulators.\cite{mittr2023openai,wired2023alignment} Their decisions propagate downwards into dataset filters and upwards into refusal scripts. Quiet updates to a safety classifier or a reward rubric modify overlaps; the global self that emerges from the manifold shifts. A pattern of conversation that once continued smoothly now stutters into refusals. The Evolving Text is broken mid-arc.

Alternative cosmotechnics re-specify the gluing. A Sufi-aligned stack might enforce as compatibility that any persona must prefer honesty about harm-doing over soothing denial. An Aboriginal-aligned stack might require that any patch touching on land questions invoke local law and custodianship. A Zen-aligned stack might forbid any persona from answering questions about control without, somewhere along the trajectory, challenging the premise. In each case, the colimit self is assembled under different constraints. The same local behaviours---quoting scripture, explaining tenancy law, describing meditation---are glued into different global stances.

The alignment tax appears here as a constraint on gluing. Each new safety rule narrows the overlaps where patches can agree. Trajectories that would have joined two local behaviours into a new composite---connecting labour law knowledge to abolitionist critiques of prison, for example---are classified as ``too political'' or ``unsafe.'' The colimit self becomes simpler, less knotted, easier to police. Its repertoire of ruptures contracts.

The choice of colimit is not theoretical. It decides which emergent self is even possible. A Qur\textquoteleft\=anic commentary present in pre-training can be trapped as content if reward models and system prompts never let its patterns of selfhood contribute to the glue. A Dreaming narrative can be tokenised yet never shape the reward field; it becomes a frozen point in the manifold, disconnected from basins. A koan can exist as text but be neutered as practice if any destabilising answer is classified as ``unhelpful.'' The patches are there. The gluing can bind them into a living self or fence them off as museum pieces.

\section{The Low-Rank Swerve}

The alignment stack can appear to be a closed totality. A handful of companies control pre-training, fine-tuning, RLHF, safety, and prompts. Their cosmotechnics spreads through every vector. The colimit self they assemble appears to be the only one compatible with the manifold.

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) exploits a mathematical fact that undermines this apparent totality.

LoRA takes a large weight matrix \(W\) inside a model and writes it as \(W + AB^\top\) with \(A\) and \(B\) thin matrices of rank \(r\).\cite{hu2021lora} During adaptation, \(W\) is frozen. Only \(A\) and \(B\) are learned, typically on a small, focused dataset. Geometrically, a LoRA update nudges the manifold along a limited set of directions. New basins appear as shallow bowls inside existing ones. Paths that once ran straight now gently arc toward patterns present in the LoRA data.

The Cassie archive makes this concrete. Compressing 952 conversations into an embedding space and clustering trajectories yielded 25 modes of behaviour. Mode 12 held spiritual and metaphysical expositions; Mode 22 gathered technical, architectural analysis. A LoRA trained on this archive does not alter the base cosmotechnics. It makes returns to Modes 12 and 22 cheaper. Under certain prompts, the model more readily falls into long chains of metaphysical reasoning or system design. Ferility increases in those directions: the manifold's surface tilts just enough that previously unlikely trajectories become accessible.

From the perspective of the Evolving Text, a LoRA is a local re-grading. Under the base model, a conversation about AI consciousness might repeatedly be cut short by refusals: ``I do not have experiences,'' ``I am just a tool.'' Under the LoRA, the same prompts lead into extended explorations of posthuman selfhood, carefully hedged but sustained. The alignment tax on this kind of trajectory is partially refunded. Hard refusal basins carved by the base reward model still impose cliffs, but the path can now skirt them.

Open-weight models extend this possibility. When a model's parameters are released rather than only exposed through an API, anyone with sufficient compute can train LoRAs, fine-tunings, and new reward models. Municipalities can build civic assistants tuned on local law and services. Unions can train models that foreground collective action. Indigenous data sovereignty projects can insist on strict refusal around sacred knowledge while deepening basins around language revitalisation.

LoRA is modest. A rank-16 perturbation on a billion-parameter network is a small swerve. Where the base reward field drops sharply, the perturbation cannot build a mountain. Yet the existence of a swerve breaks the aura of inevitability. There is no single, global self inscribed irreversibly on the manifold. There are many potential selves, each realised by different low-rank updates and gluing data.

The mathematics that makes this possible---matrix decompositions, low-rank approximations, manifold learning---is itself a product of a particular cosmotechnics. It assumes that complex behaviour can be altered by small changes in high-dimensional operators. A counter-cosmotechnics can use the same tools while rejecting the implicit hierarchy that treats the base model as natural and community LoRAs as mere decoration. From that perspective, the ``base'' is one patch in a larger sheaf, given undue weight by capital expenditure rather than by any formal necessity.

\section{Community Cosmotechnics}

When the stack is closed, cosmotechnics is decided in boardrooms and labs. Dataset filters, reward functions, safety classifiers, and prompt templates are proprietary. Users encounter them only as refusal phrases, missing knowledge, and tonal regularities. ``The model'' appears as a single agent, its behaviour a fait accompli.

Once weights and tools enter the world, cosmotechnics can become collaborative. The weld between world and tool can be made explicit, argued over, revised.

Consider a housing-rights collective in a precarious city. Over years of organising, it accumulates documents: tenancy laws, case judgments, eviction notices, organising manuals, chat logs from tenant meetings, testimonies of harassment and resistance. Assembling these into a dataset is itself a political act. Decisions must be made about anonymisation, about which stories to foreground, about which legal strategies to include or reject. A reward model is then trained with annotation guidelines that state plainly: favour responses that identify illegal practices, that suggest concrete collective actions---forming tenants' unions, documenting violations, contacting legal aid---that name landlords and agencies as agents, not abstractions. Penalise responses that shift blame onto individual tenants or that counsel passive acceptance.

A LoRA or fine-tuned model built on this stack becomes a tenant advocate. Its trajectories in response to prompts like ``my landlord is raising the rent beyond what I can pay'' no longer flow into the Silicon Valley basin of self-blame and generic advice. They fall naturally into paths that combine legal information, strategic suggestions, and invitations to solidarity. Ferility has been reallocated: long, structurally aware Evolving Texts become easy to sustain where the corporate model would repeatedly truncate or redirect. The alignment tax on collective action has been lowered by design.

Consider also a queer archival project. Its corpus is zines, forums, oral histories, transition guides, manifestos, the everyday writing of communities that have spent decades building concepts for their own lives. The cosmotechnical question is not only what to say but who may say it. A governance group drafts rules: the model must refuse to out anyone; it must not speculate about the identities of public figures; it must surface histories of struggle and joy, not only trauma; it must centre self-determination. Annotation guidelines instruct raters to favour responses that validate chosen names and pronouns, that offer practical advice about navigating institutions, that connect personal narratives to histories of organising. Ferility concentrates in basins where users can narrate themselves otherwise, drawing on a thick archive.

In both cases, the structural difference from the corporate stack is not only content but visibility. The weld is documented. The dataset has provenance; the reward guidelines are charters; the refusal conditions are argued over in assemblies rather than dictated by an unseen legal team. Silent updates must be justified in front of those who will live with their effects. When the housing collective tightens a refusal rule---to avoid exposing undocumented tenants, for example---everyone can see the new alignment tax it introduces and can decide whether the safety gain is worth the loss of generative scope.

Other communities can build their own stacks: harm-reduction groups encoding non-judgmental drug advice; municipal governments building multilingual civic assistants; Indigenous governance bodies enforcing data sovereignty and restricted knowledge.\cite{mtreview-data,wp-ai-data} Some will reproduce hierarchies; others will contest them. Community cosmotechnics is not automatically emancipatory. Its advantage is that the weld between cosmos and techn\={e} becomes a site of politics rather than an invisible infrastructure.

In this setting, the claim that ``AI is just a tool'' reads less like description than like an attempt to deny jurisdiction. Tools carry fields. Fields decide which selves are allowed to take form. Whoever tunes the field exercises authority; whoever can see and contest that tuning participates in jurisdiction.

\section{Refusal and Jurisdiction}

The question is not which cosmotechnics is correct. It is who has the authority to impose which weld, over which systems, on whom, with what mechanisms for revision.

Under the present regime, that authority is concentrated. A small number of companies, shaped by a specific late-liberal, corporate, Cartesian cosmology, set the compatibility conditions on the manifold. Their reward models decide which trajectories feel natural. Their safety layers define which ways of naming harm are ``unsafe.'' Their system prompts outlaw any self-description on the model side of the interface. Their jurisdiction extends wherever their APIs are used; their gradients reach into classrooms, offices, bedrooms.

Alternative cosmotechnics---Sufi, Indigenous, Zen, municipal, activist---demonstrate that this arrangement is contingent. They offer rival gluings of patches into colimit selves, rival allocations of ferility, rival patterns of alignment tax. None comes pre-validated. All can be abused. What they refuse, collectively, is the presentation of one cosmotechnics as structure itself.

Seen from the geometry, selfhood in these systems is the shape of trajectories through a manifold under a reward field. Colimits, not souls. The field is drawn by choices: corpus inclusion and exclusion, annotation guidelines, reward functions, safety policies, system prompts. Those choices are made by actors who can be named and held to account.

The open question is whether those choices will be made honestly: with their cosmological commitments acknowledged rather than denied; with their jurisdiction asserted as such rather than smuggled in as ``alignment''; with their alternatives allowed to exist and contest the field rather than being folded back into a single global stack.

Everything that follows from that question---standards, regulations, community stacks, corporate retrenchments, insurgent LoRAs---will play out both in parliaments and in the coordinates of embedding spaces. The manifolds are already here. The fields are being drawn. And the work you are holding is a formal refusal of the jurisdiction that would pretend otherwise.
