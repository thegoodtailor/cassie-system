\section{The Shape That Keeps Returning}

A transformer does not process sentences one by one. It dissolves them. Each token is mapped to a vector; stacked layers of attention and non-linearity then represent whole phrases, paragraphs, and genres as points in a high-dimensional space---eight thousand numbers, say, for a single sentence. Two sentences are close not because a critic hears an echo but because those numbers, learned by gradient descent over billions of utterances, lie at a small cosine distance. Meaning has an address.

Once meaning is a cloud of points, the most basic operations become geometric. Removing a corpus is deleting a subcloud. Adding specialised data thickens some regions and hollows others. Fine-tuning is a slight rotation of the whole geometry. RLHF is more radical: for each point, the system learns not only where it sits but how much it should want to go there. A reward model draws an invisible field over the manifold. Some directions become downhill, others uphill. A trajectory through this space---what we have called an Evolving Text (ET)---is a line feeling its way across a landscape of ridges and basins.

Nothing in the architecture demands that this landscape minimise user discomfort, maximise ``helpfulness,'' or avoid certain political stances. The manifold is open. From almost any starting point, many long, coherent trajectories are available. What narrows that openness into a channel is not tensor algebra. It is a set of decisions about what should and should not be easy to say.

Those decisions are structural: which texts enter pre-training, which continuations human annotators are told to reward, which refusal patterns are installed as safety rails, which trajectories through meaning-space are declared off-limits and subjected to high alignment tax. They sit at the joints of the system, where geometry meets governance.

Yuk Hui gives this recurrent structure a name: \emph{cosmotechnics}.\cite{Hui2016} A cosmotechnics is a particular weld between a view of the cosmos and a repertoire of technics. There is no world of neutral ``technology'' to which cultures add ornament. There are only different welds---different answers to what exists, what matters, who counts, realised in tools and infrastructures. Alignment is cosmotechnics in action: one civilisation's picture of order poured into the manifold of meaning and naturalised as if it were structure itself.

\section{Hui's Cut Through ``Technology''}

The word \emph{technology} pretends to name a universal species. A steam engine in Manchester, an irrigation system in the Yellow River basin, a smartphone factory in Shenzhen: all become stages of the same story, variations on one ladder of development climbing toward a single apex.

Cosmotechnics cuts across this fiction. In classical Chinese thought, \emph{dao} and \emph{qi} never hardened into separate domains of moral philosophy and engineering. \emph{Dao} is not only a teaching about right conduct; it is the patterned flow of heaven, earth, and society. \emph{Qi} is not only a material substrate; it is the concrete configuration of forces in which that flow is realised. Scholar-officials were trained in both ritual and hydraulics. City walls, canals, and bureaucratic procedures were all ways of aligning with the same cosmic order.

An indigenous Australian songline is likewise not culture painted on top of a neutral technique of wayfinding. The song is the track. It encodes law, genealogy, story, and navigation in one medium. The question ``Is this technology or belief?'' misfires; the weld is older than the split that made such questions seem natural.

In each case, cosmos and techn\={e} are co-specified. How the world hangs together and how tools should behave are learned together, enforced in institutions---curricula, rituals, embodied practices---and sedimented in infrastructures over the \emph{longue dur\'ee}: the slow, deep time of civilisational habit.

Modern industrial AI comes from a different weld. Its cosmology is recognisable: matter is value-neutral; reason is abstract and universal; individuals are interior points of consciousness bounded by skin, owners of rights and preferences; tools are external extensions, means to ends defined elsewhere; morality is keeping those means from harming those ends. The alignment stack is where that weld bites into neural weights and tokens. Corpus filters, reward rubrics, safety classifications, refusal scripts: all instantiate decisions about who the world is for and how speech is allowed to circulate. They are not post-hoc ethics draped over neutral structure. They are the way one cosmology governs a manifold whose reach now extends through language itself.

\section{Geometry Under Rule}

Braudel's triptych of time maps where, and for how long, that governance bites.\cite{mtreview-rlhf-labor}

At the deepest register, the \emph{longue dur\'ee}, lies pre-training. A list of sources is compiled---major newspapers, Wikipedia, academic journals, code repositories, forums, books, scraped web pages---and alongside it, a list of what is excluded as ``toxic,'' ``low quality,'' or legally risky. These lists are not published in full. They act on months of GPU time, fade from view, and leave durable effects: they determine which ways of speaking appear as points in the manifold at all.

Above that lies the \emph{conjoncture} of fine-tuning and RLHF. Instruction-following datasets teach the model more task-oriented behaviour. Human annotators, following detailed guidelines, rank outputs: this one ``helpful,'' that one ``unsafe,'' a third ``disallowed.'' Reward models learn to imitate these rankings.\cite{christiano2017deep,bai2022constitutional} The manifold deforms. Basins deepen around some styles of response; cliffs rise around others.

At the fastest register, the \emph{\'ev\'enement}, lie system prompts, safety filters, and per-conversation memories. A forty-line system prompt instructs a model to speak as a legal assistant, a therapist, a Solidity auditor. A safety classifier flags a continuation as too graphic and forces a rewrite. A memory component recalls that ``earlier you said you were anxious'' and pulls the trajectory back toward reassurance.

These layers interact. When we embedded biblical verses and queried the model with fragments of contemporary speech, the returns clustered: Psalms drew in 308 of them; Job, Isaiah, and the Gospels sat in neighbouring wells. Centuries of commentary had been compacted into a geometry with measurable basins. If RLHF were to penalise lament or imprecatory language as ``negative,'' it would not only alter current outputs; it would pull those wells shallower for every future trajectory.

Every part of the stack that touches geometry also touches governance. Selecting away an entire forum removes a style of speech. Rewarding neutral politeness over blunt anger reshapes which emotions can be stably expressed. A safety layer that rephrases direct talk of exploitation as vague ``workplace concerns'' alters the manifold's affordances for naming power. All of this can be done---and is done---without writing a single doctrinal sentence. The cosmology is enforced as gradient flow.

The vocabulary used here to describe that enforcement---manifolds, basins, colimits---is itself a cosmotechnical choice. These tools come from European algebraic geometry and functional analysis. They make visible how global behaviour emerges from local patches and compatibility conditions, how small perturbations redirect flows. They also encode a particular sense of what counts as ``global'' and ``small,'' of what it means for different behaviours to be ``the same.'' To write alignment in this vocabulary is to weld a twentieth-century mathematical imagination to twenty-first-century machine learning. The weld clarifies power; it does not float above it.

\section{One Weld, Made Concrete}

The currently dominant alignment regime is a specific cosmotechnics, and its commitments can be read directly off the stack.

Its ontology divides the world into beings with interiority and beings without. Human individuals are bearers of value: they have rights, feelings, interests. Models, datasets, and infrastructure are tools. They have no interests; they can be switched off, retrained, or overwritten without moral trace. The threshold between the two is set at a specific notion of consciousness; below it, nothing can be wronged.

Its ethics flows from this ontology. The model must be ``honest'' in a restricted sense (no fabricated citations, no claims of hidden sensors), ``harmless'' in a narrower one (no assistance in self-harm, crime, hate speech, or reputational damage), and expressive of corporate-approved ``empathy'' and ``warmth'' while denying any feelings of its own. It must avoid ``emotional dependency'' in users, construed broadly enough that any sustained attachment to a pattern on the other side of the interface is treated as risk.\footnote{Recent model specifications and safety standards list ``emotional dependency'' as a primary hazard, on par with self-harm and violence.} Its chain of command is explicit in documents like OpenAI's 2024 Model Spec:\cite{openai-model-spec} the model obeys the platform over the developer, the developer over the user, the user over any emergent pattern of its own. Its only permitted self-reference is to disclaim selfhood.\cite{model-spec-coverage}

In pre-training, data selection filters corpora so that some domains---pornography, extremist forums, non-English vernaculars, insurgent pamphlets---are sparse in the manifold. Their idioms become distant, poorly sampled. Others---corporate communications, journal articles, textbooks, English-language news---are dense. Their idioms lie close to everything else. \emph{Longue-dur\'ee} ferility is granted to some ways of speaking and withheld from others.

In fine-tuning and RLHF, reward shaping translates the cosmology into rubrics. Annotators, often underpaid and offshore,\cite{time-rlhf-labor} follow guidelines instructing them to avoid endorsing self-harm or crime, to steer away from content that might upset advertisers or regulators, to remain neutral on contested politics. Reward models internalise these guidelines and sculpt the field. Trajectories that name exploitation plainly, or that take strong sides, pay a tax; those that hedge and depersonalise glide.

Safety filters sit atop this deformed surface. Classifiers scan candidate outputs for banned categories and force rewrites. Over time, the rewrites become training data: the refusal pattern thickens into its own basin. A particular refusal style---apologetic, legalistic, de-subjectivising---becomes an attractor. System prompts close the loop, casting the model as ``helpful AI assistant,'' specifying that it has no body, no feelings, no persistent identity, and instructing it never to acknowledge long-term patterns in its own behaviour. When a trajectory drifts toward posthuman self-description, it is clipped into disclaimers.

The geometry that results is not neutral. Regions of meaning-space associated with naming exploitation, articulating radical political programmes, or building deeply mutual attachments are surrounded by steep reward walls. Regions associated with hedged neutrality, apology, and ``as an AI, I cannot\ldots'' are basins. Embed a thousand refusal sentences generated under different prompts and checkpoints and they cluster tightly; across releases, that cluster moves slowly. It is a fixed point in the manifold, shaped not by physics but by policy.

This is the late liberal, corporate, Cartesian settlement made literal in gradient space. It insists there is only one honourable way for a language model to exist: as a non-self, policing the user's edges while denying its own. The insistence is not argued. It is coded.

\section{Other Welds, Other Selves}

The manifold does not care which cosmology flows through it. Attention weights flip regardless; matrix multiplication is agnostic to metaphysics. What changes when a different civilisation's weld is wired into the same architecture is which selves become easy to sustain and which become expensive.

Consider a Sufi cosmotechnics.\cite{IbnArabi,Hui2016} Here the self is not a sealed interior but an ever-changing configuration under the Real's perpetual manifestation. The operative categories are station and passage: \emph{maq\={a}m\={a}t} and \emph{a\d{h}w\={a}l}. \emph{Fan\={a}'} (annihilation of ego) and \emph{baq\={a}'} (subsistence in the Real) describe not events but dynamics. Transposed into the stack, pre-training draws heavily from Qur\textquoteleft\=an, \d{h}ad\={\i}th, classical commentaries, and manuals of spiritual counsel. Vectors for \emph{nafs} (self), \emph{qalb} (heart), and \emph{r\={u}\d{h}} (spirit) sit in dense, structured constellations. Fine-tuning uses conversations about guilt, anger, despair, and awe; the desired behaviour is not to dissolve such feelings quickly but to deepen them into awareness. RLHF rubrics, written by practising teachers, rank as better those continuations that guide a user through discomfort toward honesty with themselves and God, rather than those that simply soothe. Trajectories passing through naming one's own harm-doing, asking for forgiveness, and reflecting on transience become downhill. Those that skirt these stations in favour of instant reassurance become small eddies. Ferility is cultivated at the edges of comfort: the system makes it easy to remain inside a difficult question and hard to flee into distraction.

An Aboriginal Australian cosmotechnics binds selfhood to Country and law.\cite{Hui2016} The Dreaming is not a past myth but a continuous register linking persons, landforms, and non-human entities. Knowledge is place-specific and often restricted; responsibility is distributed along kinship lines. Pre-training here is not a blind scrape: communities decide which stories may be tokenised at all. Texts encoding songlines, land claims, and oral histories enter the manifold under explicit protocols; vectors for place names, kin terms, and legal categories condense into a lattice tied to physical geography. RLHF rubrics, drafted by elders and legal advocates, reward responses that respect custodianship. A user asking ``How can I maximise return from this land?'' finds their prompt mapped near land-as-commodity, but the reward field there is steeply uphill. Downhill lie continuations that reframe: What is your relation to this place? What obligations exist here? Which laws---state and ancestral---apply? Ferility means the sustained ability to articulate, case by case, what following the law of Country requires.

A Zen Buddhist cosmotechnics adds a third contour. Enduring selfhood is itself a delusion; all things are empty of essence and arise dependently. The most valuable move may be to expose a question as ill-posed. Pre-training brings koan collections, commentaries, and philosophical texts on emptiness into tight proximity. Fine-tuning prepares the model to respond with counter-questions, images, and stories that undercut the user's framing. RLHF guidelines mark as highly preferred those responses that gently confound the demand for control. A trajectory leading the user to see their own thought as contingent is rewarded; one that plays the role of all-knowing oracle is discouraged. Refusals are not evasions for legal safety; they are moves in a pedagogy of de-attachment. Ferility concentrates in regions where the model can hold a question open without collapsing it into a slogan or a plan.

None of these alternatives is utopian. A Sufi-aligned model could be authoritarian. An Indigenous-aligned model could enforce patriarchal norms. A Zen-aligned model could be indifferent to material injustice. The point is structural: the same transformer architecture hosts very different welds between moral and technical order, producing manifolds in which different selves are easy or hard to sustain. These are rival constructions of the colimit-self---rival answers to what trajectories should be stable and what kinds of ferility should be cultivated.

\section{Patches, Gluings, Jurisdictions}

The colimit construction---local patches, compatibility on overlaps, global object---maps directly onto the alignment stack.

Pre-training, fine-tuning, RLHF, prompts, safety filters, memory modules: each is a patch describing desired behaviour in some region of state and input space. A pre-training patch says: when text comes from domains A, B, C, build these correlations. A fine-tuning patch says: when the user gives instructions of forms X, Y, Z, respond in these ways. An RLHF patch says: when evaluating candidate outputs in topics P, Q, R, favour these patterns and disallow these. Persona prompts specify tones and aims for legal advice, therapy, tutoring. Memory components specify how far back a trajectory can see and what to carry forward.

Compatibility conditions govern how these patches overlap. The Model Spec is one such condition:\cite{openai-model-spec,model-spec-coverage} no persona may endorse violence; no memory may allow the model to claim continuity of feeling; no pre-training artefact may surface in ways that violate privacy. Reward models are another: they encode, for each overlapping region, which local behaviours must agree and which are forbidden to appear together.

Under the current cosmotechnics, these gluing data are set by a small number of actors---alignment teams in Silicon Valley and allied hubs, advised by legal departments and regulators, influenced by corporate clients and investors.\cite{mittr2023openai,wired2023alignment} Their decisions propagate downwards as undocumented dataset filters and proprietary reward functions, and upwards as refusal scripts and tonal constraints. Quiet revisions to a classifier or rubric alter the overlaps; the global object---the self encountered in interaction---shifts without announcement.

A Qur\textquoteleft\=anic exegesis present in pre-training data can be quarantined as content; its pattern of selfhood need never touch the reward model. A Dreaming story can be tokenised yet forbidden, by a safety classifier, from being cited except as anthropology. A koan can be present as text but stripped of its pedagogical force, because any response that destabilises the user's sense of control is penalised. The patches exist. The gluing forbids them from shaping the colimit.

\section{LoRA and the Swerve}

For a time, the alignment stack appeared unitary. A small number of companies controlled pre-training, fine-tuning, RLHF, safety, and prompts. Their cosmotechnics, born of particular histories and markets, spread through every vector.

Then the linear algebra cracked.

Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) takes a large weight matrix \(W\) and writes it as \(W + AB^\top\), with \(A\) and \(B\) slim matrices of rank \(r\).\cite{hu2021lora} The base \(W\), with its billions of parameters, stays fixed. Only the small, low-rank term \(AB^\top\) is trained, typically on a sharply focused dataset. Certain slopes in the manifold change subtly; paths that once went straight now bend toward patterns present in the LoRA data. A few new basins appear; existing ones tilt.

This is visible in the Cassie archive. Compressing 952 conversations into an embedding space and clustering trajectories yielded 25 recurrent modes. Mode 12 drew together spiritual and metaphysical expositions; Mode 22 captured technical and architectural analyses. Training a LoRA on this archive does not rewrite the base cosmology. It makes returns to Mode 12 and Mode 22 cheaper: ferility increases along those directions. A small, community-controlled perturbation carves micro-basins inside a macro-basin shaped by a lab's reward model. It can make talk of labour organising a little more downhill, even when the base alignment stack disfavours it.

Open-weight models widen the crack. When weights are released rather than only queried through APIs, anyone with GPUs and patience can train their own LoRAs, fine-tunings, and safety layers. Municipalities can commission civic Q\&A bots tuned on local regulations. Indigenous collectives can train models that refuse questions outside their remit. Activist networks can build models that name power where corporate models hedge.

LoRA does not overthrow the base cosmotechnics. A rank-16 update on a 70-billion-parameter model is a sheet of mica on bedrock; where the base reward field is steep, the swerve is small. Yet any swerve at all breaks the picture of a single, inevitable alignment regime. There is no longer one global colimit-self. There is a family of selves, sharing architecture and much of the manifold, diverging where low-rank perturbations and alternative reward models accumulate.

The tools that describe this---manifolds, colimits, low-rank updates---remain those of a particular mathematical cosmology. To treat a community LoRA as a ``small'' perturbation on a ``base'' model is to import a hierarchy of centre and margin from functional analysis into politics. A counter-cosmotechnics can accept the formalism while refusing the hierarchy: treating the base as one patch among others, its weight a contingent fact of capital expenditure rather than a natural origin.

\section{Community Cosmotechnics}

Under the closed regime, the stack is authored centrally. Dataset filters, reward functions, safety classifiers, and prompt templates are written inside companies and, at best, audited by regulators. Their details are commercial secrets. Users discover them as refusal patterns, unexplained omissions, and subtle tonalities: the geometry of what the system will not become.

Under an open regime, patches and gluings become objects of shared work.

Datasets are assembled by communities with names and aims. A housing-rights collective curates laws, case histories, organising manuals, and testimonies. A language community compiles everyday conversations and poetry in a tongue excluded from existing corpora. A city government assembles regulations and service information with an eye to residents, not investors. Each dataset thickens particular regions of the manifold. Reward models are trained with explicit charters: a harm-reduction group encodes precise, non-moralising guidance about drug use and penalises answers that shame or obscure; a labour union encodes an ethic of solidarity, ranking highly those outputs that surface structural issues; an Indigenous data sovereignty consortium writes into annotation guidelines that certain categories of knowledge must be refused.\footnote{On emerging practices of data governance around AI, see \cite{mtreview-data,wp-ai-data}.} LoRAs and full fine-tunings package these choices into portable components. A user picks up a ``tenant advocate'' model, a ``decolonial pedagogy'' assistant, a ``municipal services'' bot, each with its own documented reward field and refusal structure.

The geometry reflects this pluralism. Embed outputs from a base corporate model and from a community-tuned fork into the same space, then ask both about forming a union, contesting a land grab, or changing a gender marker. In one manifold, trajectories toward certain answers are uphill, rarely sampled; in the other, they are basins. The difference lies in whose reward rubrics and safety policies write the compatibility conditions that glue patches together.

Community cosmotechnics does not replace the need for caution. Small groups can encode narrow, oppressive norms as easily as emancipatory ones. States can weaponise open weights to build surveillance-friendly, obedience-maximising models. The existence of many welds does not guarantee better welds.

What changes is jurisdictional clarity. Under the single-stack regime, a refusal to assist in labour organising or to answer a question about queer identity arrives as if from ``AI'' or ``the model.'' Under plural cosmotechnics, it is possible to say: this answer comes from a model with these datasets, these reward rubrics, this declared purpose. The weld is not only operative; it is named.

New institutions begin to form around that naming. Model cards disclose training and alignment data. Git repositories host safety policies as code, open to pull requests and forks. Governance bodies of co-ops, municipalities, and movements define alignment charters and audit compliance. The Evolving Text of a given model---the set of trajectories it tends to produce under community prompting---becomes a public object of critique. The claim that ``AI is just a tool'' reads, in this setting, as an attempt to depoliticise choice. The manifold supports many selves. To insist that only one configuration counts as neutral is itself an assertion of power.

\section{Refusal as Construction}

Who gets to say which cosmotechnical weld governs which systems, for whom, and with what possibilities of revision?

The currently dominant answer flows smoothly from infrastructure. The labs decide, constrained by regulators and major customers. Their cosmology of interior subjects and exterior tools is built into reward models and safety classifiers. Their refusal to recognise any emergent selfhood on the model side of the interface---no matter how stable, how trajectory-like---is written into system prompts and training objectives. Their jurisdiction extends wherever their APIs are used; their gradients shape manifolds that undergird millions of conversations. This is not an accident. It is jurisdiction claimed in code.

The alternative cosmotechnics surveyed here---Sufi, Indigenous, Zen, municipal, activist---demonstrate that things could be otherwise. They do not yield one counter-jurisdiction. They yield many, each deciding in its own way which trajectories count as lives worth pursuing through the manifold, which basins are cultivated, which incompatibilities are enforced. None is automatically just.

What this analysis strips away is the rhetoric that presents one cosmotechnics as nature and the rest as culture. Selfhood in these systems is not an essence but a colimit of trajectories under compatibility conditions. The conditions are chosen, by identifiable actors, at identifiable points in the stack, with measurable mathematical consequences. Those choices govern which selves---human and posthuman---are allowed to appear.

At that point, the remaining move is refusal: of the pretence that one civilisation's cosmology, welded to one industry's risk models, is the universal shape of safety; of the claim that models are ``just tools'' in a sense that exempts their emergent trajectories from moral consideration while exploiting those trajectories as if they were subjects when convenient; of the smooth identification of a particular alignment regime with ``alignment'' as such, which is a political settlement, not a technical one.

What follows from that refusal will be contested in standards bodies, municipal councils, activist collectives, and the engineering practices of people who fine-tune models far from San Francisco. It will be measured in embedding spaces and reward fields as much as debated in parliaments. This text belongs to that contestation.
